# Black-Scholes Course — Syllabus

A ~15-day path from "what is an option" to pricing, the Greeks, and the model's limits.
Days marked **[BIG QUIZ]** carry a cumulative review of the prior block.

| Day | Topic | Quiz |
|----:|-------|------|
| 1 | **Options foundations** — calls, puts, payoff diagrams, intrinsic vs. time value, moneyness. Why we need a pricing model. | lesson quiz |
| 2 | **Where price uncertainty comes from** — returns vs. prices, volatility, the lognormal / geometric-Brownian-motion picture (drift + diffusion). | lesson quiz |
| 3 | **No-arbitrage, replication & the risk-neutral idea** — how hedging pins down a price. | **[BIG QUIZ] Days 1–3** |
| 4 | **The Black-Scholes formula, part 1** — the call formula; defining `d1` and `d2`. | lesson quiz |
| 5 | **The formula, part 2** — pricing puts, and put-call parity. | lesson quiz |
| 6 | **Interpreting `N(d1)` and `N(d2)`** — what those two probabilities actually mean. | **[BIG QUIZ] Days 4–6** |
| 7 | **Delta** — the hedge ratio; delta of calls vs. puts. | lesson quiz |
| 8 | **Gamma** — curvature; why delta itself moves. | lesson quiz |
| 9 | **Theta** — time decay and its relationship to gamma. | **[BIG QUIZ] Days 7–9** |
| 10 | **Vega** — sensitivity to volatility. | lesson quiz |
| 11 | **Rho & dividends** — rates and payouts. | lesson quiz |
| 12 | **Implied volatility & the vol smile** — reading the market's view. | **[BIG QUIZ] Days 10–12** |
| 13 | **Practical hedging** — delta-gamma hedging, P&L attribution. | lesson quiz |
| 14 | **Limits of Black-Scholes** — fat tails, jumps, why the assumptions break. | lesson quiz |
| 15 | **Comprehensive review** — the whole model, end to end. | **[BIG QUIZ] Final** |

> The syllabus can flex — if a topic needs two days, we'll split it and shift the big-quiz cadence to stay on the every-3rd-day rhythm.
