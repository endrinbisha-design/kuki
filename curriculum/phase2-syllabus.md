# Phase 2 Syllabus — Two Tracks (Day 16+)

After the 15-day Black-Scholes core, Phase 2 runs **two parallel tracks** each day, so you're covered for both generalist S&T and derivatives/vol desks. Daily prob/EV + a brainteaser continue (they're the mental-math muscle both paths test). **Big cumulative quiz every 3rd day (Days 18, 21, 24…)** covers both tracks.

Each daily file has: Part 1 prob/EV · Part 2 brainteaser · Part 3 **Track G** lesson + quiz · Part 4 **Track D** lesson + quiz · Answers at the end.

---

## Track G — Generalist Sales & Trading

Markets fluency, products, and how a desk actually thinks — what flow/generalist interviews weight most.

| Day | Track G topic |
|----:|---------------|
| 16 | **Market-making & microstructure** — bid/ask, spread capture, adverse selection, inventory skew |
| 17 | **The macro map** — rates, growth, inflation, and how asset classes connect ("what's your view") |
| 18 | **Rates & fixed income I** — the yield curve, duration, DV01 *(big quiz day)* |
| 19 | **FX** — spot, forwards, carry, and triangular relationships |
| 20 | **Credit** — bonds vs. CDS, spreads, default risk |
| 21 | **Risk management & sizing** — VaR, stop-outs, Kelly, position limits *(big quiz day)* |
| 22 | **Reading the tape & order flow** — what informed flow looks like |
| 23 | **The behavioral / "why trading" round** — structuring your story, market-view pitches |
| 24 | **Putting it together** — a mock generalist interview *(big quiz day)* |

## Track D — Volatility Trading (Derivatives)

The natural level-2 after Black-Scholes: trading volatility as an asset, ending at dispersion.

| Day | Track D topic |
|----:|---------------|
| 16 | **The vol surface in depth** — skew & term-structure dynamics, sticky-strike vs. sticky-delta, forward vol |
| 17 | **Variance & volatility swaps** — pure realized-vol exposure; the log-strip replication |
| 18 | **Correlation & dispersion** — index vol vs. single-name vol, the correlation premium *(big quiz day)* |
| 19 | **Relative-value vol** — calendar spreads, skew trades, gamma scalping P&L |
| 20 | **The VIX & vol products** — how VIX is built, futures term structure, ETPs |
| 21 | **Exotics I** — digitals & barriers, and why they're skew/vol-of-vol sensitive *(big quiz day)* |
| 22 | **Local vs. stochastic vol** — the models behind the surface (Dupire, Heston, SABR) |
| 23 | **Skew & correlation trades in practice** — risk reversals, dispersion sizing |
| 24 | **Putting it together** — a mock vol-desk interview *(big quiz day)* |

> Flexible — if a topic needs two days we'll split it and keep the every-3rd-day big-quiz rhythm. Ask anytime to go deeper on one track, add mock interviews, or branch into a new area.
